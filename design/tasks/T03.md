## Goal
`scenes/main.tscn` (root node `Main`) is a playable click-to-move dusk street in classic 2D (pre-rendered sprites
with normal maps, StarCraft / C&C / Diablo II style), built from `layout.json`, driven by `Sim` (T01). Klas plays it
side by side with the 3D prototype (T02), so the gameplay must match it exactly (same sim, same map, same camera
angle); only the rendering differs. About two hours of work; no perfection.

## What to do
1. **Projection.** The sim runs in world metres (x, z). One small script maps world to screen for the same
   orthographic camera as the 3D game (30 degrees down, yaw 45 degrees, from +x+z), 64 px per metre:
   `screen.x = (x - z) * cos(45) * 64`, `screen.y = (x + z) * sin(45) * sin(30) * 64`, height y lifts by
   `y * cos(30) * 64`; and the inverse for mouse clicks (to the ground plane). Y-sort the world by screen y of the
   ground origin.
2. **Scene from data.** Read `res://design/layout.json` at start (`design/` has a `.gdignore`; copy the file to
   `res://data/layout.json` and load that). Ground: a big textured ground (cracked asphalt road strip, grass and moss
   elsewhere; a shader with noise is fine), drawn in screen space under everything. Props and actors from
   `assets/sprites/manifest.json` (A01, landing in batches on main): each frame a `CanvasTexture` (colour + `_n`
   normal map), the `_e` emissive layer on top, unlit. Actors pick the sprite direction from the sim heading
   (dir k = heading k * 45 degrees). Until an asset's sprites exist, a flat-shaded block of its footprint and height
   (drawn as an iso box) stands in.
3. **Camera.** Camera2D following the robot with light smoothing, about 20 m visible across (same as 3D).
4. **Play.** Left click: inverse projection -> `drone_at` -> `command_attack`, else `command_move`. A small click
   marker. Canisters vanish when taken. `advance(delta)` from `_physics_process`.
5. **Lights and shadows (the point).** CanvasModulate for dusk blue. The robot's headlight: a PointLight2D with a cone
   texture turned to the heading. Street lamps (one flickering), the campfire (flickering, warm), drones' red eyes.
   Place each light at the ground point under its source (its glow sprite at the bulb) and set its `height` for the
   normal maps. Shadows: `shadow_enabled` lights with LightOccluder2D polygons from the footprints (projected), plus a
   soft blob shadow under the robot and the drones. When the robot runs out of fuel its headlight fades out over 2 s.
6. **Atmosphere.** Drifting ground fog: a screen-space noise layer above the ground and below the HUD that lights
   brighten (a CanvasItem shader with light), plus a few slow fog sprites. `toggle_fog` (F) turns it off and on.
7. **HUD, scenario hooks, scenarios.** As in T02 sections 6 to 8 (fuel bar, messages, hint, F3 frame time/FPS/draw
   calls, R restart; `debug_move_to(x, z)`, `debug_attack(index)`; `play_loop.json` and `perf_street.json`).
