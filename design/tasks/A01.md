Pillar served: none (tech prototype; GDD section 10)
GDD refs: section 10 of `design/gdd.md`; sizes and heights in `design/layout.json` ("footprints").
Tier: 2
Look: placeholder (fast; this prototype decides 2D vs 3D, not the art)
Implementer: modeler
Branch: art/scavenger-3d-models in games/scavenger-3d, art/scavenger-2d-sprites in games/scavenger-2d

## Goal
One set of Blender models for both prototypes: glTF exports for the 3D one and pre-rendered sprites (colour +
normal map, StarCraft / C&C / Diablo II style) for the 2D one, from the same camera angle as the 3D game. Simple
low-to-mid poly placeholders with character: overgrown, crumbling concrete, moss, rust, in the spirit of
"Shumatsu Touring meets Fallout". Spend minutes per prop, not hours. No external assets (if you do use a CC0 one,
list it with URL and licence in `assets/SOURCES.md`).

## Assets (names are the file names; sizes from layout.json footprints, rot 0 = as listed)
- `robot` (player, 1.4 m): a small, boxy service robot, one headlamp eye, moss and small plants over about half its
  body. Animations: `idle` (1 frame is fine), `walk` (8 frames, loop), `attack` (6 frames, a melee swipe).
  Object-hierarchy animation is fine (no armature needed).
- `scrap_drone` (0.8 m, hovers 1.2 m up): rusty, a red eye. Animation `hover` (4 frames, loop).
- `fuel_canister` (0.5 m): a yellow jerrycan, scuffed.
- `house_ruin_a` (8 x 7 m, 6 m), `house_ruin_b` (7 x 6 m, 5 m): two-storey suburban houses, collapsed roof parts,
  crumbled concrete, exposed rebar, vines.
- `subway_entrance` (4 x 6 m, 3 m): stairs going down, rusty railing, a broken sign.
- `car_wreck` (4.2 x 1.9 m, length along x): rusted, flat tyres, grass growing through.
- `street_lamp` (4.5 m, bulb at 4.2 m): bent, the bulb as a separate emissive part.
- `campfire` (1.2 m): ring of stones, logs, embers emissive.
- `tree` (6 m, trunk footprint 1 m), `bush` (1 m).
- The ground, road and fog are made in each game (not your job).

## Shared rules
- Metres, origin on the ground at the footprint centre, glTF axes (+Y up). The robot and the drone face +X at rot 0
  (the sim's heading 0 = +x).
- Materials that survive glTF: Principled BSDF with plain values, vertex colours or baked images. Procedural node
  textures do not export; bake them if you use them.
- Sources: Blender Python scripts in games/scavenger-3d `art/blender/` (put a `.gdignore` in `art/`), run with
  `./tools/blender.ps1`.

## Deliverable 1: glTF (games/scavenger-3d)
`assets/models/<name>.glb`, animations named as above.

## Deliverable 2: pre-rendered sprites (games/scavenger-2d)
- Camera: orthographic, matching the 3D game's camera: 30 degrees down, yaw 45 degrees, looking from +x+z towards
  -x-z (Godot `Camera3D.rotation_degrees = (-30, 45, 0)`). Scale 64 px per metre. Transparent background.
- Per frame: `<anim>_<dir>_<frame>.png` (colour: albedo with baked ambient occlusion, no scene lights) and
  `<anim>_<dir>_<frame>_n.png` (normal map in screen space, Godot 2D convention: red = right, green = up on screen,
  blue = towards the viewer; check one sprite lit from the right in Godot looks lit on its right side). Optional
  `_e.png` for emissive parts (headlamp, drone eye, bulb, embers), drawn unlit on top.
- Actors (`robot`, `scrap_drone`): 8 directions, `dir` k = the actor's world heading k * 45 degrees (heading 0 = +x,
  increasing towards +z, as in the sim). Props: `dir` = rot / 90 (4 rotations, 0..3), anim `static`, frame 0.
  `fuel_canister`: rot 0 only.
- `assets/sprites/<name>/...` plus one `assets/sprites/manifest.json`: per asset the image size, the pixel of the
  ground origin in the image, the anims with frame counts, the dir count, and px_per_m.
- A short README in `art/blender/` on how to re-render everything with one command.
