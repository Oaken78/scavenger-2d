# T01 Rules core: one simulation for both prototypes

Pillar served: none (tech prototype; GDD section 8.1)
GDD refs: sections 3, 5, 6, 7, 8 of `design/gdd.md`; the map is `design/layout.json`.
Tier: 2
Look: none
Implementer: gameplay-dev
Branch: feat/scavenger-2d-rules in games/scavenger-2d, then the same files on feat/scavenger-3d-rules in games/scavenger-3d

## Goal
A pure GDScript simulation of the prototype's rules in world metres, presentation-free, that both prototypes
(`scavenger-2d`, `scavenger-3d`) run unchanged. The 2D and 3D presentations (T02, T03) only read its state, send it
commands and turn its events into effects. Keep it small and boring; speed of delivery matters more than polish.

## What to do
1. `scripts/rules/sim.gd` (`class_name Sim`, RefCounted) built from the parsed `layout.json` Dictionary:
   - Fixed internal step (60 Hz accumulator in `advance(delta)`), so results do not depend on the frame rate.
   - World plane is Vector2(x, z) in metres. Robot: pos, heading (radians, world, 0 = facing +x, increasing towards
     +z), anim state `idle | walk | attack | off`, attack phase 0..1 (for the presentation's swing frame).
   - Fuel: max 100, drains 0.8/s standing and 1.5/s walking; a canister adds 35 (capped at max) and is taken once.
   - Pathing: AStarGrid2D over the layout bounds, 0.5 m cells, blocked by the footprints (rotated by `rot`, inflated
     by the robot radius). Clicking an unreachable or blocked point walks to the nearest reachable cell.
   - Commands: `command_move(target: Vector2)`, `command_attack(drone_id: int)` (walk into 1.6 m reach, swing once
     per 0.6 s until the drone is destroyed or another command arrives), `drone_at(point: Vector2, radius: float) -> int`
     (-1 if none) for click picking. Commands are ignored once the robot is out of fuel.
   - Drones: patrol a circle around their spawn, notice the robot at 7 m, chase at 2.5 m/s (straight line is fine,
     they fly), a touch costs the robot 8 fuel at most once per 1 s, 3 hits destroy one. State per drone: id, pos,
     heading, hp, alive, `patrol | chase`, a short hit flash timer.
   - Outcome: `running | out_of_fuel | tank_stable` (all canisters taken).
   - Events for effects, a list rebuilt each `advance()`: `pickup`, `swing`, `drone_hit`, `drone_destroyed`,
     `robot_hurt`, `out_of_fuel`, `tank_stable`, each a Dictionary with `type` and `pos`.
   - The layout's lights are presentation; the sim does not touch them.
2. Unit tests (`test/unit/test_sim.gd`, GUT) of the rules, not the values: walking drains faster than standing; a
   canister is taken once and never pushes fuel over max; a path never enters a footprint; a clicked point is
   reached; a drone is destroyed by hits and stays destroyed; a drone touch costs fuel but not every frame; out of
   fuel ignores commands; `advance(1/30)` twice ends where `advance(1/60)` four times ends.
3. Copy `scripts/rules/` and `test/unit/test_sim.gd` byte for byte into games/scavenger-3d on its own branch and run
   its tests there too.
