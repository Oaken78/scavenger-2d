Pillar served: none (tech prototype; Klas compares it with T03 to pick the rendering technique)

Branch: feat/scavenger-3d-demo in games/scavenger-3d

## Goal
`scenes/main.tscn` (root node `Main`) is a playable click-to-move dusk street in real 3D, built from `layout.json`,
driven by `Sim` (T01). Klas plays it side by side with the 2D prototype (T03), so the gameplay must match it exactly
(same sim, same map, same camera angle); only the rendering differs. About two hours of work; no perfection.

## What to do
1. **Scene from data.** Read `res://design/layout.json` at start (`design/` has a `.gdignore`; copy the file to
   `res://data/layout.json` and load that). Ground: a plane with cracked asphalt road (the layout's road strip), grass
   and moss elsewhere (a simple shader with noise is fine). Props and actors from `assets/models/*.glb` (A01, landing
   in batches on main); until a model exists, a grey box of its footprint and height stands in.
2. **Camera.** Orthographic Camera3D, `rotation_degrees = (-30, 45, 0)`, about 20 m visible across, following the
   robot with light smoothing. The 2D prototype uses exactly this angle.
3. **Play.** Left click: ray to the ground plane -> `drone_at` -> `command_attack`, else `command_move`. A small
   click marker. Robot and drones turn to their heading and play idle/walk/attack/hover. Canisters vanish when taken.
   `advance(delta)` from `_physics_process`.
4. **Lights and shadows (the point).** Moonlight: a dim blue DirectionalLight3D with shadows. The robot's headlight:
   a SpotLight3D at the layout's `headlight_height`, along the heading, shadows on. The street lamps (one flickering)
   and the campfire (flickering, warm) as OmniLight3D with shadows. The drones' red eyes as small OmniLights (no
   shadow is fine). When the robot runs out of fuel its headlight fades out over 2 s.
5. **Atmosphere.** WorldEnvironment: volumetric fog (lights visible in it), a low ground FogVolume, SSAO, glow on the
   emissive parts, a filmic tonemap. `toggle_fog` (F) turns all fog off and on.
6. **HUD.** Fuel bar, the outcome messages (GDD section 7), a one-line control hint, and on `toggle_stats` (F3) the
   frame time, FPS and draw calls. `restart` (R) reloads the scene.
7. **Scenario hooks on Main**: `debug_move_to(x: float, z: float)`, `debug_attack(index: int)` (index into the
   layout's enemies), and properties the scenario can assert (fuel, canisters left, drones alive).
8. **Scenarios.** `test/scenarios/play_loop.json`: walk to the nearest canister (fuel goes up), attack the nearest
   drone until destroyed, a screenshot at the start and one with the headlight on a house, `assert_no_errors`.
   `test/scenarios/perf_street.json`: the metrics step over 10 s with every light on (the frame budget in
   `design/budgets.json`).
